# Trial lesson template — Medical-Chat
**Length:** 50 minutes  
**Output:** Long tutor script (this structure) → later PDF with white + highlighter boxes

Variables use `{{like_this}}`. Stable text stays fixed.

---

## 0. Header (tutor only)
- Student: `{{student_name}}`
- Date / Preply link: `{{date}}`
- Goal (Preply): `{{goal}}`
- Specific needs: `{{specific_needs}}`
- Lessons/month wanted: `{{lessons_per_month}}`
- Focus tags: `{{focus_list}}`
- Self-level: `{{self_level}}` | Placement CEFR if any: `{{placement_cefr}}`
- L1: `{{L1}}` | Other: `{{other_langs}}`
- Availability: `{{days}}` · `{{times}}` · `{{timezone}}`
- Externals needed this trial: [timer] [role-play cards] [reading text] [optional writing box]

---

## 1. Student detailed goal (0:00–0:03) — on screen / notes
Fill from Preply Learning needs (see screenshots pattern):
- Goal, volume, focus, level, schedule, languages, interests, free-text needs.

**Tutor line:**
> “Before we start the activities, I’ve noted what you shared on Preply: you’re aiming at **{{goal}}**, you want to focus on **{{focus_list}}**, and you wrote: *{{specific_needs}}*. We’ll use the next activities to see the clearest starting point — not to test you in a stressful way.”

---

## 2. Greeting (0:03–0:08)

**Welcome**
> “Hi {{student_name}}, welcome — and welcome to Preply. I’m really glad you’re here.”

**Tutor intro (fixed core — personalise lightly)**
> “I’m Yasmine. I teach medical English and exam communication — including OSCE-style interaction and OET-focused skills. I hold a C2 level in English, I’m a doctor-in-training myself, and I build personalised plans rather than generic lessons. My job today is to understand your goal and where communication gets hard, then propose a clear path.”

**Invite student to expand**
> “Could you tell me a bit more in your own words: what you do or study, when your exam or deadline is if you have one, and what feels hardest in English at work or in stations — speaking, structure, vocabulary, or confidence?”

*[Listen. Note pace, accuracy, clinical vocabulary, confidence.]*

---

## 3. Assessing activities (0:08–0:38)

### 3A. Reading (≈8 questions) — 0:08–0:16
- Short clinical or workplace text matched to goal (OSCE leaflet / discharge-style / guideline extract).
- 8 questions: mixed scanning + one inference.
- Tutor: time lightly; note strategy and accuracy.

### 3B. Role-play — 0:16–0:28
- Match to `{{specific_needs}}` (e.g. OSCE history taking).
- Provide **Patient card** + **Tutor (examiner) focus**.
- Success criteria on script: structure, questions, summary, empathy, pace.
- Full tutor instructions: when to prompt, when to stay silent, what to mark.

### 3C. Writing (if goal needs it) — 0:28–0:33
- Very short: referral line, or 3–4 sentence summary of the role-play patient.
- Skip or shorten if pure speaking/OSCE and time is tight.

### 3D. General English micro-check — woven into feedback
- From errors heard: 1–2 patterns only (e.g. question forms, past vs present perfect).

### 3E. Pronunciation & confidence
- Observed during 3B; use criteria checklist (see `/criteria/assessment-rubrics.md`).

---

## 4. Assessment result (0:38–0:44)
Use fixed rubrics. Spoken template:

> “Thank you — that gave me a clear picture. For **medical communication**, your working level today is around **{{working_cefr}}**.  
> Strengths: {{strength_1}}; {{strength_2}}.  
> Priority areas: {{priority_1}}; {{priority_2}}; {{priority_3}}.  
> We’ll turn those into a concrete plan.”

---

## 5. Next steps (0:44–0:50)
> “You marked about **{{lessons_per_month}}** lessons per month and you’re free **{{days}}** in **{{times}}**.  
> For your goal (*{{specific_needs}}*), I’d suggest **{{sessions_per_week}}** sessions per week for **{{weeks}}** weeks, starting with **{{week1_focus}}**.  
> After you subscribe, I’ll send a short written plan. Do you want to start with pronunciation clarity, history structure, or both in parallel?”

Offer package logic without pressure. Confirm questions.
