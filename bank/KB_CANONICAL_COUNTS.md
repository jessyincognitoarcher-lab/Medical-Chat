## Retrieval rules (engine must obey)
1. Diagnose observable bottleneck first
2. Smallest sufficient component
3. Related links ≠ automatic prescription
4. Routers route; they are not the lesson
5. No invented lessons — flag KB gap
6. Reassess same target in new context after intervention
